01 / WHO WE ARE

A practical partner.
For the whole picture.

Grandeur Olympia Global Resources Limited was established and registered in 2024, with operations beginning in 2026.

Our work spans facility management, mechanical, electrical, and plumbing services, and construction project execution. These disciplines are connected: the systems within a property shape how it works, while the way it is built affects how it is cared for.

From our base in Ikoyi, Lagos, we work with clients in Lagos, Abuja, and across Nigeria and Africa. We start by understanding the property, the priorities, and the people involved, then agree on a clear scope and coordinate the work around it.

01 / ABOUT GRANDEUR OLYMPIA

Your building.
Our shared responsibility.

From a property’s everyday upkeep to the technical systems within it, good care makes a difference. Grandeur Olympia Global Resources Limited brings facility management, technical services, and construction execution under one roof.

Established and registered in 2024, we began operations in November 2025. From our base in Ikoyi, Lagos, we support clients in Lagos, Abuja, and across Nigeria and Africa, bringing the work their properties need together around a clear scope.
